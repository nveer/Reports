    Recruit

    Good to know

    This is not about qualifying. It is about finding out. Here is what is true either way.
    You do not need a college degree
    Florida wants a high school diploma or equivalent, then a training academy.
    You have to be 19
    That is the age to be a sworn law enforcement officer in Florida. There is no rush at all.
    The academy is about six months
    Northwest Florida State College in Niceville runs it, starting each August and January.
    A two year degree skips a test
    An associate degree exempts you from the Basic Abilities Test. That is a real Florida rule.
    What it pays here
    A certified deputy at the Santa Rosa County Sheriff's Office starts at about $55,600 a year.
    What you can do right now at 17
    The Teen Driver Challenge. Free, ages 15 to 19, taught by real deputies. Ride-alongs start at 18.
